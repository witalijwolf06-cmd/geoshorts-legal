GeoShorts Publishing

GeoShorts Publishing is a private tool of the GeoShorts channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer and connects only the owner's own GeoShorts accounts. It is not offered to other people.

What it does with TikTok

  • The owner signs in to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official login (Login Kit) and grants read access only: user.info.basic and user.info.profile.
  • The tool reads open_id, username, display_name to confirm that the signed-in account is the GeoShorts account.
  • With a separate sign-in that adds video.upload, the tool can place a video the owner has approved as a draft in the owner's own TikTok inbox. It sends only the video file, no caption. The owner finishes and publishes every post personally in the TikTok app; the tool never posts directly.
  • It does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok, and it reads no videos, comments, messages, followers or statistics.
